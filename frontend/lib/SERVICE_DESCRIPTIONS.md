# CleanKart service descriptions

These are the descriptions already present in `frontend/lib/mock-services.ts`. The frontend service loader should use them when the live API returns an empty description.

1. **Foam Washing** — A rich, paint-safe foam wash that lifts everyday dirt and leaves your vehicle fresh and glossy.
2. **Interior Deep Cleaning** — A detailed interior refresh for seats, carpets, dashboard, doors and hard-to-reach areas.
3. **Steam Washing** — A careful steam clean that refreshes your vehicle interior and helps lift stubborn grime.
4. **Full Car Detailing** — A complete inside-and-out detail for a polished finish and a cabin that feels refreshed.
5. **Exterior Deep Cleaning** — A deeper exterior clean for paintwork, wheels and trim, finished with a careful hand dry.
6. **Furniture Cleaning** — Freshen up fabric furniture with professional vacuuming, stain care and upholstery cleaning.
7. **Office Cleaning** — Reliable cleaning for workspaces, shared areas and office floors, scheduled around your day.
8. **House Cleaning** — A practical home clean for floors, surfaces and everyday spaces, delivered at your doorstep.
9. **AC Services** — A technician visit for AC cleaning and service, with options for routine care or a deeper clean.
10. **Water Tank Cleaning** — A thorough tank clean with scrubbing, residue removal and a final hygiene-focused rinse.
11. **Kitchen Cleaning** — Give your kitchen a detailed clean, including worktops, surfaces and grease-prone areas.
12. **Carpet Cleaning** — Deep carpet care that targets dust and everyday marks to leave your room feeling fresh.
13. **Sofa-Dry Cleaning** — Refresh sofa fabric with careful dust removal and spot care, suited to your seating size.
